# NOVA Experimental Track Freeze — v0.1 through v0.18

**Effective:** 2026-09-15  
**Status:** `EXPERIMENTAL_TRACK_FROZEN_FOR_PRACTICAL_TRANSITION`

The primary theoretical/prototype sequence v0.1–v0.18 is frozen after completion of N-Universe Adaptive Experiment Design.

This means:

- no automatic continuation to v0.19 merely because another abstraction can be invented;
- existing experimental artifacts remain immutable/replayable;
- the track may be explicitly reopened if practical implementation exposes a real structural failure;
- reopening must name the failed invariant, evidence, and affected artifact lineage.

The freeze does **not** mean:

- NOVA is production-ready;
- NOVA is already a complete practical programming language;
- the current mathematics is terminal;
- the experiment family is universally complete.

The next primary track is:

$$
\boxed{\text{NOVA Practical Language Track}}
$$

Its initial questions are:

1. practical authoring syntax/projection for humans and AI;
2. compiler/runtime/execution/debug/interop/deployment architecture;
3. first real application primarily implemented in NOVA;
4. verified corpus design and required corpus scale.

The purpose of freezing the experimental line is to force theory to meet implementation.
